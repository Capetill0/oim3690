## Review Questions
1. Your friend's <img> won't center even though they added margin: 0 auto. What's the fix and why?
    - because it's still an inline element, need to add "display: block;" or else the image would still be a part of the paragraph.
2. AI-generated CSS has transition: background-color 0.3s paired with a :hover rule. What do they do together? Does transition only work with :hover?
    - when you hover over it it'll just follow the css, so the background color as red would show up. It'll take 0.3s to transition instead of it happening immediately.
